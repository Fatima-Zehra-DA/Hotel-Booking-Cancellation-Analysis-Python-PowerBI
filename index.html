Project Overview

From Raw Data to Business Insights

The analysis combines Python-based data preparation and exploratory analysis with Power BI visualization to investigate hotel booking cancellations.

Business Question

What patterns are associated with hotel booking cancellations, and how do cancellation rates differ across hotel types, lead-time groups, deposit types and booking channels?

Analytical Approach

The project uses data cleaning, feature grouping, exploratory data analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards to identify cancellation patterns and communicate findings clearly.
