Project Overview

Understanding Booking Cancellations

The project examines hotel booking data to identify patterns associated with cancellations across hotel types, lead-time groups, deposit types and booking channels.

Business Question

What patterns are associated with hotel booking cancellations, and how do cancellation rates differ across different booking characteristics?

Analytical Approach

The analysis combines Python-based data cleaning, feature engineering and exploratory analysis with Power BI for interactive visualization and business-focused storytelling.

Key Findings

What the Analysis Reveals

Selected findings from the hotel booking cancellation analysis.

37.13%

Overall Cancellation Rate

44K of approximately 119K bookings were cancelled.

41.71%

City Hotel

City Hotel bookings had a higher cancellation rate than Resort Hotel bookings.

99.36%

Non-Refund Deposit

The Non-Refund deposit category recorded an exceptionally high cancellation rate.

180+ Days

Longer Lead Time

Cancellation rates were substantially higher among bookings made far in advance.
